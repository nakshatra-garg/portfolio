Anatomy of a reply

A caller hears a sentence, not a token.

Text-to-speech can't start until a full sentence exists, so time-to-first-token undersells what a caller waits through. I made Time-to-First-Sentence (TTFS) our primary voice latency metric. Here's the production budget, drawn to scale.

First token (TTFT)50 ms
First sentence p95300 ms
First sentence p99600 ms
Full response p951.5–2 s
0500 ms1 s1.5 s2 s

Served on my own stack

An open-weight LLM on vLLM and NVIDIA H200s, behind a smart HAProxy router. No caller data leaves our infrastructure.

Guarded inside the budget

PII redaction, thinking-token leak suppression, repetition control and content filtering, all within those numbers.

Watched in real time

Every percentile above is tracked live on Prometheus dashboards, so regressions show up before callers notice.
